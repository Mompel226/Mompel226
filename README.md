## Why it is all free

I teach at a school with real stability and real resources. Not every school has that, and not every
teacher has the time to build their own tools on top of a full timetable.

So the point of this account is to spend the comfort I have on the people who do not: to put working,
finished things where any school can take them, and to lift some weight off teachers who are already
carrying enough. Where AI helps me build these faster, that is what it is used for — making tools
that are open and shared, not shut behind a licence.

If something here is nearly right for your school, fork it and change it. That is what it is for.

## For students

Interactive revision for Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 — four shelves, and behind each, labs where
the questions mark themselves. No sign-up, nothing to install.

|  | Start here |
|---|---|
| **At NLCS Jeju** | **[nlcsbiology.com](https://nlcsbiology.com)** — the revision hub, plus the school's own co-curricular activities, societies, Bryant and community enterprises. Sign in with your school account and it also carries your assessment record. |
| **Any other school** | **[igcse-biology-hub](https://github.com/Mompel226/igcse-biology-hub)** — the same revision hub with the NLCS-only parts taken out. Yours to host, yours to change. |
